zero-tabbox

Every tab closes at your cutoff. Settings save as you change them.

The contract

At each cutoff every tab closes. Nothing is saved. Bookmark what matters before the cutoff.

There is no undo, no archive and no restore — that is the whole point. This page opens by itself only once; reach it later from the extension menu.

Cutoff times

Between 1 and 4 times a day, in 24-hour local time.

Notice and exemptions

Minutes of badge countdown, 0–60. Set 0 for no warning.
One notification when the countdown starts. No snooze.
The only exemption. Pinned tabs survive every sweep.

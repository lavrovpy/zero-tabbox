zero-tabbox

Every tab closes at 18:00.
Nothing is saved.

If a tab matters, bookmark it. If you don't, it's gone at 18:00. Read the five terms before you keep the extension.

  1. Every tab in every normal window closes — audio, groups and discarded tabs included.
  2. Nothing about them is stored. Not the URL, not the title, not the favicon.
  3. There is no undo, no archive, no snooze and no per-tab protection.
  4. Closing the browser does not dodge it. A missed cutoff runs at the next start.
  5. No data leaves the browser. Nothing is collected, nothing is sent.

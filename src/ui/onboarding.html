zero-tabbox

Every tab closes at 18:00.

If a tab matters, bookmark it before the cutoff. Read the terms before you keep the extension.

  1. Every tab in every normal window is included — no undo, no snooze, no per-tab exceptions.
  2. Quitting the browser doesn't dodge it. A missed cutoff runs at the next start.
  3. No data is collected — everything stays on your device.
